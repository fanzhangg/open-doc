Paper and ink

OpenDoc's design system. The page is quiet so the writing and the buddies stand out: a warm grey ramp, one ink, and colour only where it means something.

This page is rendered with the app's own web/app.css. Change the system there and here together.

Rules for changing the UI

For anyone, person or agent, who changes how the app looks.

  1. Reuse before adding. Use an existing class (.od-primary, .od-secondary, .od-icon-btn, .od-input, .od-segmented, .od-switch, .od-panel…) before writing a new rule.
  2. Colours are tokens. No hex, rgb(), oklch(), black or white outside the token blocks at the top of app.css. A colour with a new meaning gets a token there, and a line on this page, first.
  3. Colour means something. Grey for the UI; ink for the one primary action and for what is on or selected; a buddy's own colour only for that buddy; red for danger and deletions; green for insertions and online; yellow for commented text; blue only for focus, text selection and links.
  4. Sizes come from the scales. Font sizes 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 24px; weights 400, 500, 600; radii 0, 4, 6, 10, 14px or full. Spacing is on a 4px grid.
  5. One primary button per view. Everything else is secondary, ghost or a link button.
  6. Sentence case. Labels and headings are never all caps.
  7. Both themes. Check light and dark; tokens flip on their own, so a change that needs a dark-only override is usually using the wrong token.

test/design-system.test.js fails on colour literals and off-scale sizes, radii and weights in web/app.css and desktop/start.html.

Buddies

Each buddy has its own colour, the one in its face (see Avatars). It is set as --buddy on whatever belongs to that buddy: its @chip, edits, flag, typing dots and the card it is working on.

Avatars

Everyone has a face, drawn from their name by web/faces.js, so it is the same on every machine and nobody picks or uploads one. The faces below are drawn by that code.

Buddies: a body, a face and a colour

A buddy is one of five bodies (circle, square, triangle, pentagon, cloud) with a face: eyes (dot, big, happy, sleepy, squint), a mouth (smile, grin, o, flat, cat) and maybe one extra (blush, glasses, bow). Its colour is one of eight OKLCH hues, evenly spaced, with Clippy's sunflower first. Outlines are a little uneven and tilted, as if drawn by hand, and the same every time.

In a doc, buddies take parts and colours as unlike each other's as possible: the first five never share a body, eyes or a mouth, and eight never share a colour. These are eight buddies in one doc:

Clippy

Clippy's face is fixed: a sunflower cloud with dot eyes, a smile and blush. It is also the app's mark, on its icon and its documents.

People

People are always round. Their face and colour come from their name, the same way as a buddy's.

Sizes

Set by --size on .od-avatar. Features are drawn to read at 18px; a new eye, mouth or extra must too.

States

A buddy shows what it is doing with its face, not with a badge: working, its body rolls and its face bobs; queued, it breathes; offline or starting, it greys out. A green dot means it comments on its own.

Faces have their own palette and gradient in faces.js, the one place outside the tokens with colour literals. Ink for features is #1f1d24.
