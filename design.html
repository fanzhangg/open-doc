Paper and ink

OpenDoc's design system. The page is quiet so the writing and the buddies stand out: a warm grey ramp, one ink, and colour only where it means something.

This page is rendered with the app's own web/app.css. Change the system there and here together.

Rules for changing the UI

For anyone, person or agent, who changes how the app looks.

  1. Reuse before adding. Use an existing class (.od-primary, .od-secondary, .od-icon-btn, .od-input, .od-segmented, .od-switch, .od-panel…) before writing a new rule.
  2. Colours are tokens. No hex, rgb(), oklch(), black or white outside the token blocks at the top of app.css. A colour with a new meaning gets a token there, and a line on this page, first.
  3. Colour means something. Grey for the UI; ink for the one primary action and for what is on or selected; a buddy's own colour only for that buddy; red for danger and deletions (a buddy's deletions are grey); green for insertions and online; yellow for commented text; blue only for focus, text selection and links.
  4. Sizes come from the scales. Font sizes 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 24px; weights 400, 500, 600; radii 0, 4, 6, 10, 14px or full; spacing 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px; control heights 24, 28, 32px (36 and 40 for bars and big buttons).
  5. One of each control. Icon buttons are .od-icon-btn: 28px, 32 in the header, 24 inside a row. Menu and list rows share one size, set once in app.css: 28px tall, 8px side padding, radius 6, 13px; grey on hover, --active when selected or current.
  6. One primary button per view. Everything else is secondary, ghost or a link button.
  7. Sentence case. Labels and headings are never all caps.
  8. Both themes. Check light and dark; tokens flip on their own, so a change that needs a dark-only override is usually using the wrong token.

Buddies

Each buddy has its own colour, the one in its face (see Avatars). It is set as --buddy on whatever belongs to that buddy: its @chip, edits, flag and the card it is working on. A run's edits show in the text as a revision: new text in its colour, removed text grey and struck through where it was.

Type

Inter for the UI. The Markdown editor's page font (sans, serif or mono, set in that view) and its sizes in em are the writer's, not the UI's. A text file that is not Markdown shows as it is, in --mono at 14px by default (a size from 12 to 20 in its plugin).

Fields and choices

14 px

A stepper is a .od-segmented with .od-stepper: − value +, for a whole number in a range. A view's or rule's settings are rows of a switch, a segmented choice or a stepper; the first row of a group has a heading, .od-setting-group: 12px, semibold, faint.

Settings with a page of their own

A group of settings with its own page, such as a view or a rule, is a row in a list: its name, what it is and whether it is on, the whole row one button with a chevron. Its page starts with .od-back, the same Back as the history's.

Lists: current is ink on grey

A sidebar is resized by dragging its edge: the cursor turns to col-resize and the edge shows a 2px --text-faint line; double-click the edge for the default width back.

Status bar

The plain view has a status bar under the page: .od-statusbar, 28px, 12px text in --text-soft on --bg-soft, a hairline --border on top. The caret's place is on the left; on the right text buttons, 22px, grey on hover and ink on grey while pressed or open, and a menu above them in the .od-block-menu style. Line numbers, whitespace marks (dots for spaces, arrows for tabs) are --text-faint; indent guides are --border-strong.
